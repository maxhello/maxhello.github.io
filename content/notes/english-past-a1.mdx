**预习**:对应多邻国 A1 第 52 到 53、63 到 66 单元,加第 67 到 70 单元的零件,当前学到第 34 单元。这是 A1 最后一个大句型。例句来自官方单元讲解。

## 0. 总规则套在这里

和现在时一模一样地分两路:有 be 就用 be 的过去式 was/were;没有 be 就用 did。

| | 有 be 动词 | 没有 be 动词 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 陈述 | I **was** a manager. | I **lived** in Brazil. |
| 提问 | **Was** he angry? | **Did** you like magazines? |
| 否定 | He **was not** angry. | I **didn't** call the doctor. |
| 疑问词 | What **were** your responsibilities? | Where **did** the accident happen? |

did 出场后,动词回原形,这和 does 的规则一样:Did you play(不是 played),I didn't need(不是 needed)。

## 1. be 的过去式:was / were(第 52、53 单元)

| 主语 | 过去式 | 例句 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I / he / she / it | was | I was so angry. / My cat was under the bed. / The fridge was empty! | 我当时非常生气。/ 我的猫在床下。/ 冰箱空了! |
| you / we / they | were | The kids were in the kitchen. | 孩子们在厨房。 |

| 否定 | 缩写 | 例句 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| was not | wasn't | The window wasn't closed. / The dog wasn't outside. | 窗户没关。/ 那只狗不在外面。 |
| were not | weren't | The dogs weren't dirty. | 那些狗不脏。 |

提问把 was/were 提前:Was the fridge empty? / What were your responsibilities?

## 2. 规则动词过去式:加 -ed(第 63、64、65 单元)

过去式不随主语变,所有人称一个形式。

| 原形 | 过去式 | 例句 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| play | played | I played golf on Saturday. | 我星期六打了高尔夫。 |
| watch | watched | He watched golf yesterday. | 他昨天看了高尔夫。 |
| rain / snow | rained / snowed | It rained, so we needed an umbrella. / It snowed a lot in December. | 下雨了,所以我们需要伞。/ 十二月下了很多雪。 |
| stay | stayed | He stayed home. | 他待在家里了。 |
| live / move | lived / moved | I lived in Brazil as a child. / I moved here years ago. | 我小时候住在巴西。/ 我几年前搬来这里。 |

**拼写**:一般加 -ed;以 e 结尾只加 -d(love → loved);辅音 + y 结尾变 y 为 i 加 -ed(study → studied,cry → cried)。

**现在时 vs 过去时**:

| 现在时(习惯) | 过去时(特定事件) |
| --- | --- |
| He watches golf a lot. | He watched golf yesterday. |
| We camp in May. | It rained a lot last May. |
| She paints every day. | She painted pictures every day.(过去的习惯) |

## 3. 否定:did not / didn't + 动词原形

| 例句 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| I didn't need advice. | 我不需要建议。 |
| I did not call the doctor. | 我没有打电话给医生。 |
| I didn't text anyone. | 我没有给任何人发短信。 |

**注意**:didn't 已经表示过去,后面动词回原形。I didn't call,不是 I didn't called。

## 4. 提问:Did + 主语 + 动词原形

| 一般疑问 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| Did you play tennis every day? | 你以前每天打网球吗? |
| Did she do karate? | 她以前练空手道吗? |
| Did you like magazines? | 你以前喜欢杂志吗? |
| Did the robber wear black clothes? | 抢劫犯穿黑衣服吗? |

| 特殊疑问 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| Where did the accident happen? | 事故发生在哪里? |
| Did you call an ambulance quickly? | 你很快叫了救护车吗? |
| Oops, did I make a mistake? | 哎呀,我犯错了吗? |

## 5. 同期零件

**比较级 -er than(第 67 单元)**,句型 A + is + 形容词-er + than + B:

| 规则 | 例子 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 直接加 -er | long → longer | Tom is funnier than Ben. |
| 以 e 结尾加 -r | safe → safer | Rap is cooler than rock! |
| 以 y 结尾变 i 加 -er | funny → funnier | This crowd is bigger. |
| 辅音+元音+辅音结尾双写加 -er | big → bigger | |

**最高级 the -est(第 68 单元,课程只用不讲)**:That actor is the coolest. / Which athlete is the oldest?

**someone / anyone / something / anything(第 70 单元)**:肯定句用 some-,否定句用 any-,疑问句两个都行。

| 肯定 | 否定 | 疑问 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I see someone! | I didn't text anyone. | Did you see anyone? |
| I can do something! / My grandfather noticed something too. | I can't hear anything. | Can I ask something? |
