## 一、每天看的规则(一屏)

**看句子里有没有 be 动词(am / is / are),提问和否定都靠它分路。**

| | 有 be 动词 | 没有 be 动词 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 陈述 | She **is** tall. | She **likes** coffee. |
| 提问 | **Is** she tall? | **Does** she like coffee? |
| 否定 | She **is not** tall. | She **does not** like coffee. |
| 疑问词 | Where **is** she? | What **does** she like? |

1. 有 be:提问把 be 提到最前,否定在 be 后加 not。不用 do/does。
2. 没 be:提问用 Do/Does 开头,否定用 don't/doesn't。he/she/it 用 does,其他人用 do。
3. Does / doesn't 出场后,后面的动词回原形。Does she like,不是 Does she likes。
4. 疑问词(What / Where / When / How much)放最前面,后面照样走上面两条路。
5. 陈述句里 he/she/it 的动词加 -s;以 -s/-sh/-ch/-x/-o 结尾加 -es(watches / goes)。
6. I 用 am;have 到 he/she/it 变 has,但 Does she have 不变。
7. 多个东西:名词加 -s,be 用 are。How much 后面 is 还是 are 看东西单复数。

## 四、参考表(想查再看)

### 1. be 动词:am / is / are

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I am... | I am happy. | 我很开心。 |
| He/She/It is... | She is tall. | 她很高。 |
| You/We/They are... | They are happy. | 他们很开心。 |

**记忆**:I 用 am;第三人称单数 he/she/it 用 is;you 和复数(we/they)用 are。I 也是单数,但它不用 is。

**缩写**(口语和多邻国里大量出现):

| 完整 | 缩写 |
| --- | --- |
| I am | I'm |
| he is / she is / it is | he's / she's / it's |
| you are / we are / they are | you're / we're / they're |
| is not / are not | isn't / aren't |

I'm late! / He's funny. / We're from London.

### 2. be 动词的提问和否定

| 陈述 | 提问(be 提前) | 否定(be 后加 not) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The coffee is hot. | Is the coffee hot? | The coffee is not hot. |
| The eggs are good. | Are the eggs good? | The eggs are not good. |
| I am a tourist. | Am I a tourist? | I am not a tourist. |

Is this your passport? / Are those your shoes? / No, I'm not surprised.

**记忆**:单数主语用 is,复数主语用 are。be 的否定不需要 do/does。

### 3. 一般现在时:do / does

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I/You/We/They + 动词原形 | I like coffee. | 我喜欢咖啡。 |
| He/She/It + 动词-s | She likes coffee. | 她喜欢咖啡。 |
| Do + 主语 + 动词原形? | Do you like coffee? | 你喜欢咖啡吗? |
| Does + 主语 + 动词原形? | Does she like coffee? | 她喜欢咖啡吗? |

**重点**:does 后面的动词用原形。Does your foot hurt?(不是 hurts)

**第三人称单数加 -s 还是 -es**:动词以 -s / -sh / -ch / -x / -o 结尾加 -es,其余加 -s。

| 原形 | he/she/it |
| --- | --- |
| like / cook / play | likes / cooks / plays |
| wash / watch | washes / watches |
| go / do | goes / does |

He watches TV at night. / Emma washes the windows. / Our family often plays games.

### 4. 一般现在时的否定:don't / doesn't

| 主语 | 否定结构 | 例句 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I / You / We / They | do not (don't) + 动词原形 | I don't have a pet. | 我没有宠物。 |
| He / She / It | does not (doesn't) + 动词原形 | Emma doesn't read maps. | Emma 不看地图。 |

We don't play soccer. / I don't understand the rules. / Sorry, I do not live here.

**注意**:肯定句 Emma read**s** maps,否定句 does 已经带了第三人称,动词回到原形 read。

### 5. 疑问词 Wh-

结构看动词类型,还是那条总规则:

| 动词类型 | 结构 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| be 动词 | Wh- + be + 主语? | Where is she? / Who are they? |
| 其他动词 | Wh- + do/does + 主语 + 动词原形? | What do you want? / When does the store open? |

| 疑问词 | 中文 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What | 什么 | What is this? / What do you like? |
| What + 名词 | 什么颜色 / 什么…… | What color is Sara's hair? / What color does she like? |
| Who | 谁 | Who is she? |
| Where | 哪里 | Where is the bathroom? / Where do you live? |
| When | 什么时候 | When does the store open? |
| Why | 为什么 | Why are you sad? |
| How | 怎么样 / 如何 | How are you? / How are your parents? |
| How much | 多少钱 | How much is this hat? / How much are the desserts? |

**注意**:What is this? 里没有 do/does,它走的是 be 动词那条路。How much 后面跟 is 还是 are 看物品单复数。

### 6. There is / There are

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| There is + 单数 | There is a cat. | 有一只猫。 |
| There are + 复数 | There are two cats. | 有两只猫。 |
| Is there...? | Is there a cat? | 有猫吗? |
| Are there...? | Are there any cats? | 有猫吗? |

**记忆**:一个用 is,多个用 are。any 用在疑问句和否定句里(Are there any cats? / There aren't any cats.),肯定句用 some(There are some cats.)。

前 34 个单元的语法讲解里还没正式讲这个句型,先留着,遇到再回来看。

### 7. Have / Has

| 句型 | 例句 | 中文 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I/You/We/They have | I have a dog. | 我有一只狗。 |
| He/She/It has | My mom has blue eyes. | 我妈妈有蓝眼睛。 |
| Do you have...? | Do you have a pet? | 你有宠物吗? |
| Does she have...? | Does she have a pen? | 她有笔吗? |
| don't / doesn't have | I don't have a pet. | 我没有宠物。 |

**注意**:Does she have,不是 Does she has;否定用 doesn't have,不是 hasn't。

### 8. This / That / These / Those

| 单数 | 复数 |
| --- | --- |
| This is a book. 这是一本书。 | These are books. 这些是书。 |
| That is a book. 那是一本书。 | Those are books. 那些是书。 |
| this pen 这支笔 | these pens 这些笔 |

近处:this / these;远处:that / those。Is that your watch? / Are those your shoes? / Those boots are so big!

### 9. 物主:my / your / his / her / its / our / their

| 英语 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| my book | 我的书 |
| your bag | 你的包 |
| his car | 他的车 |
| her dog | 她的狗 |
| its tail | 它的尾巴 |
| our house | 我们的房子 |
| their children | 他们的孩子 |

英语里物主词什么名词都能带,包括无生命的:my bag / my gate / my phone。

Tom's book = Tom 的书,Sara's hair = Sara 的头发。这里的 's 表示所属关系,不是 is。its(它的)没有撇号,it's = it is。

### 10. 名词复数与冠词 a / an / the

**复数**:一般加 -s;以 -s / -ss / -sh / -ch / -x 结尾加 -es。数量大于一,名词必须用复数。

| 单数 | 复数 |
| --- | --- |
| pen / dollar / key | pens / dollars / keys |
| dress / toothbrush | dresses / toothbrushes |
| one pen | two dresses |

**冠词**:

| 规则 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| a + 辅音音素开头 | a banana, a tomato, a pizza |
| an + 元音音素开头 | an apple, an egg, an onion |
| the + 特指(单数或复数都行) | the burgers, the bathroom, the bus station |
| 不可数名词不加 a/an | I need meat. / I love rice! |
| 运动名称前不加冠词 | We play tennis. / They like baseball. |

### 11. 频率副词与时间介词

**频率副词放动词前**(usually / always / often / sometimes),**时间放句末**(every day / at night)。

| 例句 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| I usually rest. | 我通常休息。 |
| He always washes the windows. | 他总是擦窗户。 |
| I sometimes go to the gym. | 我有时去健身房。 |
| Tom cooks every Friday. | Tom 每周五做饭。 |
| He watches TV at night. | 他晚上看电视。 |

**时间介词**:具体时刻用 at,星期几用 on(星期常用复数表示"每逢")。

| 用法 | 例子 |
| --- | --- |
| at + 时刻 | at seven a.m., at eight a.m. |
| on + 星期 | on Mondays, on Saturdays |
| 两个都有 | at ten a.m. on Tuesdays |

I cook at seven a.m. / I'm busy on Mondays. / I always go shopping on Saturdays.

### 12. 方位介词 in / on / under / near

| 例句 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| The toy store is in the mall. | 玩具店在商场里。 |
| Your sandwich is on the couch! | 你的三明治在沙发上! |
| My pencil is under that notebook. | 我的铅笔在那个笔记本下面。 |
| The market is downtown, near the park. | 市场在市中心,公园附近。 |

| 英语 | 中文 |
| --- | --- |
| I'm good, thanks. | 我很好,谢谢。 |
| No, thanks. | 不用了,谢谢。 |
| Excuse me, where is...? | 打扰一下,……在哪里? |
| Nice to meet you! | 很高兴认识你! |
| I'd like a coffee. | 我想要一杯咖啡。 |
| I need a new jacket. | 我需要一件新夹克。 |
| I want water! | 我要水! |
| I am going to work. | 我正要去上班。 |
| I like... / I don't like... | 我喜欢…… / 我不喜欢…… |
| ..., right? | ……,对吧?(句末反问) |
| Julia is good at this card game. | Julia 擅长这个纸牌游戏。 |
