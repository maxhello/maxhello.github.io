This is the first post in a monthly series about learning English. The [English page](/english) on this site shows the numbers every day; this post is the story behind them, written at the end of each month. I keep the English simple on purpose — it is part of the practice.

## The month in numbers

- **Active days:** 31 of 31 — no missed day in August
- **Lessons:** 540 (about 17 per day)
- **XP:** 25,337 (about 820 per day; lifetime total 26,986 by Aug 31)
- **Time:** 2,197 minutes — about 36.5 hours, or ~71 minutes per day
- **Best day:** Sunday, Aug 23 — 35 lessons, 1,660 XP, 137 minutes

By weekday, the strongest days were Wednesdays (4,448 XP) and Sundays (4,350). The lightest were Tuesdays (2,940).

## What I notice

The numbers come from the data; this section is how the month felt.

My vocabulary is clearly bigger. In daily life, I sometimes just know the English word for a thing I see. In Duolingo, my accuracy on multiple-choice and fill-in-the-blank questions is clearly better than before.

Listening is still weak. Questions that show only Chinese and ask me to write English are still hard for me. Grammar is a weak point, and my pronunciation is probably not accurate either — I was on the Super plan this month, not Max, so there was no AI speaking practice.

## Course progress

- **Units:** 13 done by mid-August, 24 by the end of the month. The Intro section was already finished; I am now 14/30 units into the first A1 section.
- **Score:** 11 → 15. The Duolingo score is CEFR-aligned and goes up to 129 in the current path. I got four bumps this month, one every 3–5 days.
- **Streak:** 21 → 36 days (it started on Jul 27; day 30 was Aug 25).
